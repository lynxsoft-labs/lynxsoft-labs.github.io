With the recent migration of the Campsis ecosystem to the dedicated **[campsisverse](https://github.com/campsisverse)** organization complete, I wanted to take a moment to share an update on my professional path and what lies ahead for the project.

### Transitioning Forward

After a fantastic multi-year collaboration, my professional relationship with Calvagone has come to a close, and I no longer work with them. I want to express my gratitude for the journey we shared and the milestones we achieved together in advancing modeling and simulation tools.

However, this transition opens an exciting new chapter. I am fully committed to remaining an independent software engineer and consultant, with a primary focus on maintaining, supporting, and further extending the **Campsis** suite. 

### What This Means for Campsis Users

- **Active Development & Support:** The open-source packages (`campsis`, `campsismod`, `campsisnca`, `campsismisc`, `campsisqual`, `campsistrans`) will continue to receive active maintenance, bug fixes, and feature expansions.
- **Independent Governance:** Operating independently ensures that the ecosystem remains open, transparent, and driven by the needs of the pharmacometrics community.
- **Consulting & Collaboration:** I am available for independent consulting, custom feature development, training workshops, and quantitative analysis projects centered around PK/PD simulation and reproducible R workflows.

### Get in Touch

Whether you are looking to integrate Campsis into your clinical workflow, require tailored simulation solutions, or simply want to discuss open-source pharmacometrics, I would love to hear from you. 

You can connect with me directly via my **[LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/)** or explore our repositories on the **[campsisverse GitHub organization](https://github.com/campsisverse)**. Thank you for your continued support as we build the future of open-source simulation tools together!

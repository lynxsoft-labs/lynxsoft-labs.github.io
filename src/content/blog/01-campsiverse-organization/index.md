As part of the ongoing evolution of the Campsis ecosystem, all open-source packages are being migrated from the original Calvagone GitHub organization to the dedicated **[campsisverse](https://github.com/campsisverse)** organization.

### Why We Are Doing This

- **Independent Open-Source Home:** Transitioning to `campsisverse` creates a focused, vendor-neutral hub dedicated entirely to open-source pharmacometric modeling, simulation, and NCA tools in R.
- **Unified Ecosystem:** Housing all core packages (`campsis`, `campsismod`, `campsisnca`, `campsismisc`, `campsisqual`, `campsistrans`) under a single organization simplifies maintenance, improves CI/CD pipelines, and unifies documentation across the suite.
- **Long-Term Governance & Maintenance:** This move ensures long-term sustainability, community transparency, and clearer distinction between open-source tools and external services or products.

*Note: Special thanks to Calvagone for supporting the transition of the Campsis ecosystem to its independent open-source organization.*

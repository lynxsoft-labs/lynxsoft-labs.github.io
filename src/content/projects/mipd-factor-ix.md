### Overview

During surgical procedures, maintaining optimal factor IX plasma levels in hemophilia B patients is critical to prevent severe bleeding while avoiding unnecessary over-dosing. 

This project involved developing a specialized **Model-Informed Precision Dosing (MIPD)** application designed to assist clinicians and pharmacometricians in personalizing recombinant factor IX (rFIX-FP) dosing schedules prior to and following surgery.

---

### Key Technical & Functional Highlights

- **Clinical PK Integration:** Implemented individual dosing optimization based on population pharmacokinetic (popPK) models tailored to rFIX-FP.
- **Precision Dosing Interface:** Engineered an intuitive interface for inputting patient demographics, baseline factor levels, and target surgical coverage profiles to simulate optimal bolus or continuous infusion regimens.
- **Workflow & Visualization:** Developed real-time simulation output views showing predicted plasma concentration-time profiles alongside target therapeutic windows.
- **Reproducible Architecture:** Designed modular computational workflows ensuring rapid Bayesian updates and robust execution.
